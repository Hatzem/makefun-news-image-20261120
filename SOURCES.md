## 1. Russia hits Kyiv hours after the peace summit (Europe / war)
- Russia launched a massive overnight ballistic-missile and drone attack
  on Ukraine into Friday morning, lasting more than six hours, hours after
  President Vladimir Putin left Shenzhen following the first-ever
  trilateral summit with U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President
  Xi Jinping. Three bridges on the Dnipro in Kyiv were damaged
  (Podilsko-Voskresenskyi, Southern, Darnytskyi), disrupting traffic
  between the capital's two banks. At least five people were killed in
  total — three in Kyiv and two in Lviv — and fires broke out in several
  Kyiv districts. Russia's defence ministry said it hit military, energy,
  port and transport targets.
- The summit itself was on Thursday 19 Nov: the three leaders issued a
  symbolic "peace declaration" reaffirming that "a nuclear war cannot be
  won and must never be fought" and promising a dialogue on reducing
  nuclear risks, with no arms-control commitments and no mention of
  Ukraine or Iran. The declaration is context here, not the story — the
  strike is the 20 Nov event.

## 2. Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in India (weather / Asia)
- Cyclone Akvan crossed the north Andhra Pradesh and south Odisha coasts
  close to Gopalpur between 07:30 and 08:30 IST on Friday, with winds of
  70–80 km/h gusting to 90 km/h, after weakening from a severe cyclonic
  storm. It is the third cyclone to hit India's east coast in under a
  month. Odisha deployed 423 rescue teams and set up 9,341 cyclone
  shelters; heavy rain fell across coastal Andhra Pradesh and Odisha, with
  red alerts in Srikakulam and Vizianagaram districts. No damage or
  casualties had been reported by Friday afternoon; the storm was expected
  to weaken into a deep depression and then a depression as it moved
  inland.

## 3. Asian markets plunge as AI bets unwind (markets / Asia)
- Asian stocks fell sharply on Friday as investors unwound AI-related
  bets: Taiwan's Taiex tumbled 6.23% (down 2,394 points to 36,033),
  Japan's Nikkei 225 fell 5.06%, South Korea's Kospi 5.21%, Shanghai
  2.0% and Hong Kong's Hang Seng 2.4%. The Taiex's weekly fall of about
  17% was its worst week since 1991, and the MSCI Asia-Pacific index was
  on track for its worst week since 2020. Chip stocks led the fall
  (TSMC, Samsung, SK Hynix, SoftBank, Advantest); TSMC dropped below
  NT$2,000. Anthropic's record $81 billion IPO — the largest listing on
  record, priced at $83 a share — did not restore confidence in AI
  trades, and the Dow had fallen 1,101 points on Thursday.
- Sources:

## 4. UK–EU reset: 100,000 youth visas (Europe)
- At a UK–EU summit in Brussels on Friday, Prime Minister Andy Burnham
  was set to sign a package of deals representing the biggest change to
  Britain's relationship with the EU since Brexit: an end to checks on
  British food and farm exports to the EU, a link between the UK and EU
  emissions-trading systems, and a youth-mobility scheme allowing
  100,000 young people a year each way to live, work and study for up to
  three years. The 100,000 figure was settled after the EU rejected
  Britain's proposed 80,000 cap (Britain had originally proposed
  50,000). The summit had been postponed from July after the resignation
  of Burnham's predecessor Keir Starmer.

## 5. GTA VI smashes launch records (culture / tech)
- Grand Theft Auto VI launched on Thursday 19 Nov on PlayStation 5 and
  Xbox Series X|S; the day-one tallies were first reported on Friday
  20 Nov. Sensor Tower estimates the game sold about 24.6 million copies
  and took in about $2.36 billion in its first 24 hours — nearly triple
  GTA V's first day. Streams Charts measured 125.47 million hours
  watched across Twitch, YouTube and Kick in the first 24 hours, with a
  combined peak of 7.91 million concurrent viewers (Twitch 3.31 million
  — the platform's biggest game category ever, passing League of Legends'
  2022 World Championship final; YouTube 3.22 million; Kick 1.5 million).
  These are third-party estimates: Rockstar and Take-Two had not
  announced official launch-day figures. (CNN reports a higher hours
  figure, over 132 million, from a later reading; the image uses the
  Streams Charts figure.)

## 6. Saudi intercepts Houthi missile (Middle East)
- On Friday morning the Saudi-led coalition said it had intercepted a
  ballistic missile and drones fired toward the kingdom's southern region,
  issuing early-warning alerts for Jazan, Abha and Khamis Mushait before
  declaring the danger passed. It came a day after a Houthi projectile
  struck Riyadh's 80-storey Public Investment Fund Tower on Thursday
  19 Nov, damaging several floors (windows blown out on about five to
  eight floors) with no injuries reported; the Houthis claimed four
  operations that day, including the financial centre, King Khalid
  International Airport, the SADAF petrochemical complex in Jubail and
  King Khalid Air Base. It was the second hit on Riyadh's financial
  district in under two weeks, after the Aramco tower on 8 Nov.
